Your day, intelligently organized.

Plan less. Focus better. Recover faster.

Problem

Most planning apps assume a perfect day: nothing gets missed, nothing runs long. Real days don't work that way — so plans get abandoned instead of adjusted.

Solution

RoutineRise treats a missed task as normal, not a failure. It detects real scheduling conflicts, offers realistic replacement time slots instead of silently rewriting your day, and keeps a recommendation ready for "what should I do right now."

Features

Tasks with priority, category and recurrence · conflict-aware scheduling · focus timer with session history · habits with a forgiving streak model · goals with milestones · daily/weekly analytics · notes · light and dark themes · local export/import backup.

Technology

A single self-contained web app — HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, no account required. All scheduling math uses native Date arithmetic so it stays correct across midnight and daylight-saving changes.

Privacy

Nothing is sent to a server. Your plan is stored only in this app's local storage on your device — not encrypted, not shared, not analyzed. You can export a backup or clear your data at any time from Settings.

Accessibility

Keyboard-navigable controls, visible focus outlines, minimum 40px touch targets, reduced-motion support, and status shown with text and icons rather than color alone.

Roadmap / known limitations

Not yet built: a full month calendar view, drag-and-drop rescheduling, multi-timezone support, and cloud sync across devices. Quick Add understands simple phrasing ("tomorrow", "at 7pm", "for 90 minutes") but isn't a full natural-language parser yet.
